## Found on the way

- The balloon's `y` leaked past the `n` widening: with `spr` widened, the bob
  `y = start + sin(offset) * 2` runs on one arm of `spr == 22` only, so a
  stored `y = start` had two successors (`start` and `[6, 10]`) and every
  state existed twice (col-census: `balloon[2].y` 2 values, 312k rows each).
  Now a phase: `start +- 2` (`PhaseRange::AroundStart`, `BALLOON_BOB_RAW`).
  Level 0 `r0sxhnp` f34: 624,224 -> 312,112 states (exactly half), wave
  3.7 s -> 1.2 s. Room (0,1) (two balloons) probably carried it too.
- Split frame on this room (`r0sxhnp`, before the balloon fix): kernels
  16k bodies (largest 393) against 176k (largest 21.6k), ~13 us a lane
  against ~80, but 2.8-3.8x MORE states at the same frame (step 68: 1.72M
  against f34's 624k) and growing faster. Two big shapes at one frame
  boundary hold the same game states with every heap pointer from the
  player's type functions on shifted by one; the lower one holds only
  just-dashed states (`dash_effect_time` 10): the freeze path's extra
  `__frozen = {}` allocation. Aliasing it (`__frozen = __phase`) made two
  traced frames hash alike (kernel build refused). The fix belongs in the
  boundary's heap renumbering. Open; this room runs unsplit.
