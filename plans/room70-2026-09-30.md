# Room (7,0) "800 m" (2026-09-30, Philippe hands-off)

Branch `step5-split`. Split frame (`CELESTE_SPLIT_FRAME=1`, two steps a frame),
as room (6,0). The room: spawn at (8,112) on the left; three fall floors next
to it at x 48-64, y 112; a fourth at (96,104) under the spring at (96,96); two
on the right wall at (112,56), (112,64); the balloon at (48,32); the exit at
the top left.

## The reference: TAS8, 84 frames, balloon seed 0

`tasdatabase/classic/any/TAS8.tas`: header `[0,]`, 61 inputs. The header is
the balloon's offset: the TAS tool (gonengazit/UniversalClassicTas) sets each
balloon's `offset` instead of `rnd(1)` (its README, "RNG manipulation"). So
TAS files fix the draw, and `pico8_diff/replay.py --balloon-seeds 0` now does
the same. In the original cart at prologue offset 23 (input 0 on frame 24,
the first frame the player exists) it exits during frame 84, every replay;
under PICO-8's own `rnd` only some replays do. Fitted to celeste-minimal by
`rewrite trajectory` and verified on a real PICO-8 with seed 0:
`tas/room_7_0_reference_frame_84.txt`.

Our search keeps `rnd` an interval at every level (decided with Philippe for
room (5,0), 2026-09-20): a confirmed horizon is "some phase trajectory
wins", the best case over draws. A result under 84 needs a witness replayed
with ONE seed before it counts.

## Built: the floors' TIMERS level (`t`, `FloorsPrecision::Timers`)

Between `b` and exact: every floor's `state` and `collideable` stay exact;
only the countdowns - each floor's `delay` and the balloon's `timer` - are
stored as the whole 16.16 range (`widen::widen_floor_timers`,
`runtime2::FLOOR_TIMER_RANGE`, the same projection in `Rt2::widen_to`). An
idle floor never reads its `delay` (a break sets it), so an untouched floor is
exact; a broken one keeps its phase (shaking / hidden) and forgets only how
far in: its `delay <= 0` is then an interval comparison, which the split
(`Domain::split_compare`) forks into two exact-`state` outcomes. The cart only
compares the countdowns with 0 and decrements them, so the whole range is as
precise as any tighter one, and it is closed under the decrement (a first try
with [-1024, 60] failed its own containment premise: the split does not narrow
the stored interval, so `-1` left the range). Ladder: `r0sxht, r0sxh, r1sxh ..
r15sxh, rxsx`; the reference engine refuses a timers level (witness at exact).

Kernels: 472,592 bodies, 2.37M fused nodes (exact: 13,526 / 1.0M); every
floor's split is enumerated in every kernel (largest 8,193 bodies = 2^13), the
build 190 s and 51 GB peak.

## `mget` outside the map (fixed)

At step 118 the timers forward aborted in a kernel: `mget out of range`.
`spikes_at` loops `for i=max(0,flr(x/8)),min(15,..)`; the branch-free kernel
unrolls it and evaluates the second column for every lane, which for a player
at x >= 119 is room column 16, map x = 128. PICO-8's `mget` returns 0 outside
the map; `CartData::mget` and `zn_mget` now do the same (a fractional
coordinate still refuses).
