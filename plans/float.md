# Float: the game in the TAS tools' doubles (`--arith float`)

The game's arithmetic is a setting. `fixed` (the default) is PICO-8's
16.16, the real game. `float` is the Lua doubles of the community's TAS
tools, in which their files are made and checked. Every piece of the
pipeline has a float counterpart: the oracle, the tracer, the graph, the
kernels, the keys and trees, the arcs, level -1, the concrete search. A
DUAL search finds the best file valid in both. Fixed stays byte-identical
(its pins, gates and trees); a tree records its arithmetic and is never
read under another. The full design history is the float design doc in
git (deleted 2026-10-16; last at `fafb3bf7`).

## The semantics and their authority

**Celia** (gonengazit/Celia, float mode) is THE float tool: `--arith float`
is Celia from its IL load (its loading jank `J`, `starts::celia_jank`,
unless `--loading-jank`) with its seeds. **UniversalClassicTas** (UCT,
`--arith float-uct`: the IL load, no jank) is a secondary report. Both run
the original cart in LÖVE 11.5's LuaJIT on x86-64, and compute the same:

| the cart's | the tools compute (`celeste_core::lua_f64`) |
|---|---|
| `+ - * /`, unary `-` | IEEE binary64, round to nearest even, in Lua's order; no FMA, no reassociation (LuaJIT's folds are bit-exact) |
| `a % b` | `a - floor(a / b) * b` (three ops) |
| `flr`, `abs` | `math.floor`, `math.abs` |
| `min(a, b)`, `max(a, b)` | `a < b and a or b`: on a tie (`-0` against `0` included) the SECOND argument |
| `sin(x)` | `-math.sin((x * math.pi) * 2)`, the host's glibc `sin` |

MATCH EXACTLY OR RAISE: a non-finite result (overflow, division by zero,
NaN) has no counterpart we model; the checked operations return `None` and
the callers raise. Bit-exact is defined on this machine's glibc (2.42);
other libms are the robustness report's question (below).

**The oracle.** `trace::oracle`'s tests replay the tools' exact traces
(every number of every object as a hex float, after `_update` and `_draw`)
through the float reference engine, BIT for bit, every update:
`gates/uct/` and `gates/celia/`, 166 goldens each (every tasdatabase file
of any%, nodiag, 100%, gemskip any%, gemskip nodiag, gemskip 100%, and two
random files a level), regenerated by `tools/{uct,celia}/golden.py`. Our
unsubmitted witnesses are LOCAL goldens (/var/tmp/float-goldens, never
committed). The original cart buffers a jump press for 4 frames, the
traced cart does not: community files are re-timed to their firing frame
(`tools/uct/canonical_jumps.py`) before they are goldens.

**One cart for both.** `move` is the original's three statements with the
hint `__split_by_round(rem)`. The `_update` guard (an object at speed 0
skips `move`) stays; where it differs from the original (an object at
speed 0 inside a solid; a `-0` remainder the original turns `+0`), no
golden meets it. The balloons: a float tree holds a balloon's phase as the
`rnd` interval unless `--balloon-seeds` fixes it; the tools' seeds (every
seed 0 without a list) are the CONCRETE runs' (`Settings::concrete`: the
concrete and dual searches, the known-route check, `l1 check`, replays),
keyed through `concrete::lookup_view`, as fixed does under
`--concrete-balloon-seeds`.

## The parameter

A compile-time choice: `celeste_core::arith::Arithmetic` (`Fixed`,
`Float`), its numbers `EngineNum` (`Pico8Num`, `LuaF64`: a code is the raw
bits, `u64` for a double), the graph's rules `transpile::garith::GraphArith`,
the kernels' lanes `compiled::KernelLanes`, the remainder model
`search::rem::Rem` (`P8`, `LuaF64`: the transfers), the circle its points
are `search::arcs::Circle` (`Fixed16`, `rem_f64::Doubles`: the hulls, the
arcs' sets). Fixed is every default type parameter.

## The remainder model and the round fork

One axis, one frame: `r1 = fl(r + ox)`, `k = floor(fl(r1 + 0.5))`,
`r' = fl(r1 - k)`.

- The remainder stays in `[-1/2, 1/2 - 2^-53]` after any move: `r1 - k` is
  exact but at `r1 = 1/2 - 2^-54`, where `k = 1` and `r' = -1/2`.
- THE ROUND FORK. `__split_by_round(v)` splits `v` into the fragments on
  which `flr(v + 1/2)` is one integer. In 16.16 that is the floor fork of
  `v + 1/2` shifted back (exact). In doubles `+ 1/2` rounds, so the fork
  cuts at the exact points: `c_k = k - 1/2` except `c_1 = 1/2 - 2^-54`,
  found by the operation itself (`Arithmetic::round_fragment`; the graph's
  `Fork`/`Frag`/`ForkOk` of `Cut::Rounds`, the kernels' `round_cut`). A literal splits
  into trace states (`Domain::literal_fragments`), as a literal floor fork.
- A transfer is `Mono(ox, k)` (`r -> fl(fl(r + ox) - k)`) or `Const`, on a
  guard (an order-interval of doubles) found exactly by bisection with the
  operation. It is monotone, not a rotation. A point is a double's
  total-order key; W is exact in the remainder, as in fixed: the PULL of a
  segment is a segment, computed exactly.
- The forward is the HULL forward (option (b)): a state's remainders as an
  arc per axis. Float holds 1.1-1.3x fixed's states, all of the gap the
  "sliver" (pieces that reach the doubles just below 1/2); the arcs and the
  concrete search are exact in the remainder, so the sliver costs size,
  never soundness. Room (1,3) peaks at 1.36x (f42) and falls to 1.20x (f46).

## Intervals, folds and the levels

- Intervals need NO outward rounding: for a monotone op the rounded
  corners bound the rounded values (`fl` is monotone); `sin` of an
  interval is `[-1, 1]`. Corners are taken in the total order (`-0` below
  `0`). A box `[0, 0]` holds `-0` lanes too, so it never folds to the
  constant `+0` (`GraphArith::alone_in_its_value`); a pixel or a world's
  value is pinned as its value box (`garith::value_box`).
- Folding is bit-identical only (`x+0`, `x*0`, `(x+a)-a` are refused). One
  exception, exact by argument: `(x + c) - x -> c` where `x` is an integer
  input (a platform's `x`: every world's is whole, checked) and `c` an
  integer or a literal with integer ends (`Symbolic::exact_sum`).
- The level flags `h f n p` work in float. Their hooks (near floors,
  platforms and their worlds, literal pins) are generic; the containment
  proof (`widen::within`) runs on exact doubles. At `n` a balloon is
  widened whole (timer, sprite, bob and phase: plans/abstractions.md).
- Level -1 is built in the run's arithmetic: ranges in the total order, a
  range grown twice jumping to `±f64::MAX`, fork spans in the arithmetic's
  own count (rounds for a round fork), whole-pixel hulls by `as_i16`. Float
  tables built in every room of the Celia goldens but the platform rooms
  and the summit, which fixed refuses too; room (1,0)'s start d is 44, as
  fixed's.
- The objects ladder (`--level r0sxhn,r0sxh`) and `check known` run in
  float.

## The kernels' safety

The float kernels are the same graph, lowered to `vaddpd`-class AVX-512 on
8 doubles a zmm (the body runs twice per 16 lanes).

- **MXCSR** is read before every float kernel call: round to nearest, no
  FTZ, no DAZ (`asm::check_mxcsr`), else fatal.
- **No FMA**: the assembled text is scanned for `vfm*`/`vfnm*` and
  `ldmxcsr` and refused (`jit::refuse_forbidden`); gcc assembles it, never
  compiles it.
- **min/max**: compare and blend, the second operand on a tie (LuaJIT's
  `min`/`max`, not `vminpd`'s).
- **sin**: a per-lane call-out to the reference's `LuaF64::lua_sin` (the
  same glibc symbol).
- `NoWrap` is FINITE: an operation that can leave the finite doubles is
  its own error, and a lane in error is a fatal coverage gap.
- Checked as in fixed: `check ref`, `--key-check`, `check widen`,
  `check arcs`, `missed 0`; the float trees of (1,0) to f44 and (1,3) to f46
  equal the float reference engine's, frame by frame (`ckhash --edges
  --hulls`). The kernel mix census (`--kernel-mix`, `bench frame
  --metrics`) is 16.16's only.

## The dual search and rule (b)

`rewrite dual` (`search::dual`): the shortest file valid in BOTH
arithmetics. Philippe's rule (b): the file's length is the FLOAT exit
(Celia's: the count), and real play (16.16, PICO-8) must leave by then.

- Each half runs its levels to the same horizon: the fixed half under the
  run's settings, real play from `--loading-jank J` (the category's boot
  chain's `J`, `pico8_diff/chain.py entry_jank`), in `DIR/fixed`; the float
  half from the float tool's own start (`--float-half celia|uct`), in
  `DIR/float`.
- A DUAL STATE is (the fixed half's exact state or EXITED, the float
  half's exact state). Under an input both reference engines step, their
  `rnd` leaves paired; a successor is admitted iff every live half lies in
  its own W. A fixed half that wins waits as EXITED; a float win is the
  dual win only with the fixed half exited, else pruned.
- Dedup and the depth-first memo are on the FULL dual state; an input is
  skipped only where both halves' read buttons agree with one run; the
  bound is the larger arc bound; a depth-first try at it, then the
  breadth-first layers to the horizon: exhaustive inside both W, so the
  first win is the dual optimum.
- The witness is replayed in both arithmetics before it is written
  (`dual::replay`), and recorded in `result.json` (`dual`).
- `--save-marks DIR` writes the float half's arc pass and the dual
  witness's path; `export-ui` reads every tree in the arithmetic its record
  names, a dual record's levels named by half.

## The robustness report

`rewrite robust --arith float --inputs FILE` (`search::robust`): the
witness replayed under the run's float tool and seeds with glibc's `sin`,
MPFR's correctly rounded `sin` (`libmpfr.so.6`, loaded at run time), every
`sin` call one ulp up, every call one ulp down, and 8 runs moving each call
by -1, 0 or +1 ulp at random (`lua_f64::with_sin_nudge`). A variant whose
first win differs from glibc's is FRAGILE: flagged, not rejected. Run so far
on the four validation witnesses only; all four are ROBUST.

## Validation

Every witness is checked in every tool: `tools/uct/check_uploads.py`
(PICO-8 from the IL load, from the previous room, from the boot; UCT; Celia
float) and Celia `--fixp` (`tools/celia/validate.sh --fixp`); the
tool file starts on the player's first frame.

- **nodiag 1800m** (room (1,2), real play J3, Celia J4): the dual optimum
  is the 85 of plans/results.md (113 frames from the load), valid in real
  play, Celia float and Celia `--fixp`; UCT, which has no loading jank,
  kills it at keypress 70. Its float optimum is the same 85.
- **gemskip any% 2600m** (room (1,3), J8): the community's 123 is the
  FLOAT optimum (the float arc bound is 149 frames, which it meets), and is
  float-only: PICO-8 and Celia `--fixp` kill it.
- **gemskip 100% 2500m** (room (0,3), J1): the submitted 116 is
  fixed-only: UCT and Celia float kill it (a ~0.0001 drift from frame 21
  flips a rounding at frame 63).
- The dual optima of 2500m and 2600m, and 2500m's float optimum, are
  unsubmitted results: they live with the local witnesses, not here.

## Open gaps

- The kernel mix census (`--metrics`) is 16.16's: no float metrics pins.
- Level -1 tables do not build for the platform rooms (6,0), (2,1), (1,2),
  in fixed either; `l1 check` ran over the 94 Celia goldens outside them,
  and over one UCT golden only.
- At `n`, `check ref` and `check arcs` skip most rows over the path cap (the
  reference forks a widened object per path), in both arithmetics; object
  rooms are checked at `r0sxh`.
- The robustness report has not been run on the earlier float witnesses.
- `-0`/`+0` are different codes (exact); a canonicalization is deferred
  until a proof that no read tells them apart.
- The diagnostics `census` and `trajectory` are fixed-only.
- Speed buckets (T3) and exact platform remainders (T2) were planned in
  case float trees ran away; they have not (where compared, float holds
  1.0-1.4x fixed's states).
